title=Survivor Memory
description=[h1]Ever walked into the same house twice?[/h1]
description=You know the feeling.
description=You open the same bathroom cabinet. Again.
description=You check the same kitchen. Again.
description=And somewhere around the third identical suburban house, you start wondering:
description=[i]Have I already been here?[/i]
description=Survivor Memory gives your character something surprisingly useful in the zombie apocalypse:
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucashw68/SurvivorMemory/main/assets/runtime/memory-status.png[/img]
description=[h2]A memory.[/h2]
description=[b]Your survivor doesn't know everything.
description=They remember what they've seen.[/b]
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]REMEMBER WHERE YOU'VE BEEN[/h1]
description=See when you first found a place, how often you have visited it, and how long it has been since you last came back.
description=Your survivor remembers the rooms they actually entered and the containers that genuinely appeared in the loot interface. Each building moves naturally from [b]Visited[/b] to [b]Partially Searched[/b] or [b]Searched[/b] as those memories grow.
description=A compact status indicator tells you where that memory stands. Click it whenever you need the full reminder.
description=[i]Yes, you already looted that bathroom.[/i]
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]YOUR MEMORIES, ON THE MAP[/h1]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucashw68/SurvivorMemory/main/assets/workshop/screenshots/survivor-memory-world-map.png[/img]
description=The World Map can display a discreet overlay for remembered buildings. Toggle it when you need it, hide it when you do not.
description=It is calculated from your survivor's memories and does not replace or pollute your manual map annotations.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]PLACES THAT MATTER[/h1]
description=Some remembered buildings become more than another dot on the map. Mark one manually as your personal [b]Home[/b] or [b]Outpost[/b] while keeping its real exploration status.
description=[b]HOME[/b]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucashw68/SurvivorMemory/main/assets/runtime/map-home-marker.png[/img]
description=The place your survivor calls home.
description=[b]OUTPOST[/b]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucashw68/SurvivorMemory/main/assets/runtime/map-outpost-marker.png[/img]
description=A useful foothold away from home.
description=Both receive their own World Map icon. Right-click a Survivor Memory marker to mark or clear the designation. Once a Home or Outpost is fully searched, the contextual memory indicator can quietly disappear without forgetting the place.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]EMOTIONAL MEMORY[/h1]
description=Some places really are harder to forget. An exceptionally severe and sustained encounter can leave a personal emotional memory of that location.
description=Coming back later may cause one small vanilla panic/stress reaction. The memory weakens with time and safe returns: no trauma points, no sanity system, no new moodle, and no permanent effect.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]THINGS WORTH REMEMBERING[/h1]
description=Your survivor can remember a few discoveries genuinely worth keeping in mind: generators, gas pumps, and antique ovens or wood stoves.
description=These memories always mean [i]last seen here[/i], never [i]still here[/i]. They are formed when the object is actually seen; Survivor Memory never searches the world remotely to check whether it stayed there.
description=No, this is not an index of every hammer, nail, or suspiciously precious can of beans.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]VEHICLE MEMORY[/h1]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lucashw68/SurvivorMemory/main/assets/runtime/map-vehicle-marker.png[/img]
description=Remember where your survivor last meaningfully encountered a vehicle: when entering it, inspecting its mechanics, or getting out.
description=Legitimate dashboard and mechanics observations can also leave broad memories of fuel and overall condition, without storing suspiciously perfect percentages.
description=The marker is the vehicle's [i]last known position[/i]. There is no live GPS, no route tracking, and no magical update if somebody moves the car while you are gone.
description=[i]Because "where did I leave the car?" is apparently still a problem after civilization ends.[/i]
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]MEMORIES AGE[/h1]
description=Visits update when your survivor returns. Emotional memories weaken with time and safe returns until they fade away.
description=Important objects and vehicles remain last-seen observations. The world may change while your survivor is away, and their memory may become outdated without pretending otherwise.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]NO MAGICAL KNOWLEDGE[/h1]
description=[b]It only records information your character has actually discovered.[/b]
description=Survivor Memory does not reveal:
description=[list]
description=[*]unexplored rooms;
description=[*]unseen containers;
description=[*]remote changes made while your survivor was away;
description=[*]information your survivor could not reasonably know.
description=[/list]
description=If someone moves something while you are gone, your survivor does not magically get a notification from the universe.
description=And no, the mod will not tell you where every can of beans in Kentucky is.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]MULTIPLAYER[/h1]
description=Memories belong to each character.
description=If Player A searches a house, Player B does not magically inherit that memory. Your friends still have to remember things themselves.
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h1]FEATURES[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]First visit, last visit, and visit count for remembered buildings.
description=[*]Rooms entered and containers actually inspected.
description=[*]Visited, Partially Searched, and Searched states.
description=[*]Compact recall panel and contextual status indicator.
description=[*]Toggleable, non-destructive World Map overlay.
description=[*]Personal Home and Outpost designations.
description=[*]Rare, fading Emotional Memories from severe experiences.
description=[*]Last-seen memories for selected important objects and vehicles.
description=[*]Broad observed vehicle fuel and condition details, with no live tracking.
description=[*]Native Build 42 options for every major feature and map category.
description=[/list]
description=[h2]OPTIONS[/h2]
description=Survivor Memory uses Build 42's native [b]Options → Mods[/b] screen. Enable only the memories and map categories you want, choose the marker size, adjust emotional reactions, configure the recall shortcut, and select your preferred UI.
description=Turning something off never erases your character's existing memories. They simply wait quietly until you enable it again. Unlike the zombies, they are patient.
description=[h2]UI & COMPATIBILITY[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Made for Project Zomboid Build 42.
description=[*]Complete vanilla UI included.
description=[*]Optional NeatUI integration when NeatUI is active.
description=[*]No required UI framework.
description=[*]Character-owned persistence in single-player and multiplayer.
description=[*]Configurable shortcut, marker filters, and marker size.
description=[/list]
