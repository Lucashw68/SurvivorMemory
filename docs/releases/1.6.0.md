# Survivor Memory 1.6.0

## Steam Workshop changenote

Survivor Memory 1.6.0 greatly expands what your survivor can remember:

- Mark remembered buildings as personal Homes or Outposts.
- Severe encounters can leave fading Emotional Memories using vanilla panic and stress.
- Remember selected generators, gas pumps, and wood stoves actually seen in the world.
- Remember where vehicles were last encountered — never their live position.
- Configure every major feature through Build 42's native mod options, including the recall shortcut, map filters, and marker size.
- Play in English, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, or German.
- Improved World Map markers, status refresh, NeatUI integration, and the complete vanilla UI fallback.
- Fixed important objects being remembered while outside the visible screen area.

Your survivor still remembers only what they actually experienced. No remote scans, no live GPS, and no omniscient cans-of-beans database.

## Manual QA

- Open `Options → Mods → Survivor Memory`; verify every option, persistence
  after restart, the configurable shortcut, marker filters and marker sizes.
- Check both NeatUI enabled and the vanilla fallback.
- Confirm the selected game language is reflected in the UI.
- Approach a gas pump from outside the viewport and confirm it is remembered
  only after it appears on screen.
- Review the two English Workshop screenshots before publication; no debug UI
  may be visible.
